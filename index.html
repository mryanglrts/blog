✦ hi, i'm mary
i've always been a pretty private person — i don't usually share much online, but lately i've wanted to leave a little trace of who i am somewhere on the internet.

a while ago, my partner and i talked about starting a blog. my first response was "nah." but if i'm being honest, part of me always wanted a space for my extremely strange dreams, random observations, and my newly developed obsession with surplus treasure hunting. that last one is entirely his fault — he has a talent for spotting vintage things in forgotten places, and somewhere along the way he pulled me in too. i'd like to think we're collectors of stories.

the funny thing about being human is that we're always changing. one year it's a new hobby, the next it's something completely different. this blog will probably reflect that. feel free to stick around.

welcome to my little corner of the internet.

◇ book i finished (a summary)
days at the morisaki bookshop — satoshi yagisawa
well, what can i say about this book? first of all, it made me teary-eyed.

i love how takako is unhinged and sarcastic. i love the dynamic between her and her uncle, and i love how momoko (the uncle's wife) is such a strong additional character.

the book is subtle, wholesome, and most of all, human. i also love how different people with different personalities just make sense with one another, creating a lively community.

(no spoilers!)

rating: ★★★★☆ (4.3/5)
